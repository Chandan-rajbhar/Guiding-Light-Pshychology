Anxiety & Stress Management

Anxiety & Stress

Anxiety may show up as worry, perfectionism, restlessness, or difficulty relaxing. I work with you to identify patterns, strengthen coping strategies, and support your nervous system in finding greater balance.
Anxiety and stress can appear in many forms, including overthinking, irritability, or feeling constantly overwhelmed. These experiences often affect sleep, focus, relationships, and overall well-being, and therapy provides a space to understand their root causes and how they impact daily life.

Common Signs of Anxiety & Stress

Stress is a normal part of life, but ongoing anxiety can begin to affect your daily functioning. You might notice:
  • Persistent worry or overthinking
  • Feeling restless or on edge
  • Difficulty relaxing or sleeping
  • Trouble focusing or making decisions
  • Irritability or emotional overwhelm
  • Physical symptoms such as muscle tension or fatigue

How Therapy Can Help

I provide a supportive and non-judgmental space where you can explore what may be contributing to your anxiety or stress. Together, we work to identify triggers, understand thought patterns, and develop healthier ways of responding to challenging situations. The process is collaborative and focused on helping you regain a sense of control and clarity.
